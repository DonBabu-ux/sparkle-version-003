## 🫧 What is Sparkle?

**Sparkle** is a modern campus-first social platform designed around **proximity, presence, privacy, and authentic interaction**.

Instead of optimizing everything around followers, likes, virality, and endless scrolling, Sparkle creates a more intimate digital environment where your **campus is the centre of gravity**.

Think:

```text
        PEOPLE
          ✦
     ┌────┴────┐
     │ SPARKLE │
     └────┬────┘

   ▼      ▼      ▼
 CAMPUS  PEOPLE  MOMENTS
   │      │      │
   └──────┼──────┘
          ▼
       COMMUNITY
```
