# Sparkle Marketplace Guide

Category: Marketplace

## How Sparkle Marketplace Works
Sparkle Marketplace allows verified campus members to buy and sell products transparently:
- **Browse & Filter**: Filter by category (Laptops, Smartphones, Electronics, Subleases, Books, Fashion), price range in KES, and specific university campus.
- **Create Listings**: Tap **+ Create Listing**, upload up to 20 product photos, specify title, price in KES, item condition (New, Like New, Good, Fair), and safe campus meetup spot.
- **Wishlist / Saved Items**: Tap the heart icon on any listing to save it to your personal wishlist for quick access later.
- **Contact Sellers**: Tap **Message Seller** on any listing card to initiate a direct chat with the seller.
- **Meetup Confirmation**: Once an item is inspected and paid for in person, confirm the order status inside Marketplace Chat.
