# Sparkle Marketplace Rules & Safety Policies

Category: Marketplace Policies

## Selling Rules & Prohibited Items
Sparkle Marketplace maintains strict safety guidelines to protect all buyers and sellers:
- **Transparent Pricing**: All listing prices must be clearly quoted in KES (Kenyan Shillings).
- **Prohibited Items**: Illegal substances, weapons, counterfeit goods, stolen items, pirated software, and academic dishonesty tools are strictly forbidden.
- **No Misleading Listings**: Stock photos without actual item images or fake pricing will result in listing removal and account suspension.
- **Communication Safety**: Keep all deal negotiations inside Sparkle Marketplace Chat to maintain a verified communication log.

## Safe Campus Meetups
- Always meet during daylight hours in populated campus areas (e.g. Student Center, Main Library entrance, Campus Security gates).
- Inspect the physical product, test electronics, and verify functionality before completing payment.
- Confirm order meetup status inside Marketplace Chat.
