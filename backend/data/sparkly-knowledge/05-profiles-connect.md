# User Profiles, Verification & Connect

Category: Profiles & Connect

## Profile Management
Every Sparkle account includes a personalized profile:
- **Profile Customization**: Update display name, bio, campus location, profile avatar, and background cover.
- **Account Verification**: Verified badges (AI, Official, Verified Creator) indicate authenticated community members and trusted sellers.
- **Sparkle Connect**: Discover students and sellers on your campus based on mutual interests and active listings.
- **Privacy Controls**: Control who can send direct messages or view your saved items.
