# Sparkly AI Assistant Overview & Features

Category: Sparkly AI Bot

## What is Sparkly Bot?
Sparkly Bot is the official AI Marketplace Assistant built directly into Sparkle Marketplace (`/sparkly-bot`). It helps users discover products, check campus prices, understand selling rules, and navigate Sparkle features seamlessly.

## Key Capabilities of Sparkly Bot
- **Smart Product Discovery**: Ask Sparkly for items like *"find laptops under 50k"* or *"cheap smartphones"*, and Sparkly will return live Marketplace listings formatted as interactive cards.
- **Sparkle Policy Guidance**: Get instant, verified answers about how to sell, create listings, contact sellers, and safe meetup spots.
- **Dynamic Time & Date**: Ask Sparkly for the current time or today's date.
- **Wishlist Integration**: Ask Sparkly to show your saved Marketplace items.
- **Controlled Memory**: Sparkly remembers user preferences (such as preferred price range or category interests) to tailor recommendations.
- **Zero-Hallucination Pricing**: Factual listing data (prices, stock, seller names) comes strictly from verified live databases.
