# Sparkle Marketplace & App Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Category: FAQ & Help

## Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: What is Sparkle?
**A**: Sparkle is the campus-first social networking and marketplace platform for students and creators.

### Q: How do I sell an item on Sparkle Marketplace?
**A**: Open Marketplace, tap **+ Create Listing**, add photos, set a title, price in KES, category, and campus meetup location, then publish.

### Q: How do I contact a seller?
**A**: Open any listing card and tap **Message Seller** to start a direct Marketplace chat.

### Q: How do I save a listing for later?
**A**: Tap the heart icon on any listing card to save it to your wishlist. Ask Sparkly *"show my saved listings"* to view them anytime.

### Q: Is selling on Sparkle free?
**A**: Yes, creating standard listings on Sparkle Marketplace is free for students.

### Q: What is Sparkly Bot?
**A**: Sparkly Bot is your AI assistant inside Sparkle that helps you search products, check selling rules, and get answers.

### Q: How do safe meetups work?
**A**: Always meet during daylight hours in public campus spots like the Student Center or Library entrance. Inspect products before making payment.
