# Sparkle Platform Overview

Category: Sparkle Platform

## What is Sparkle?
Sparkle is a vibrant campus-first social networking and marketplace platform designed to connect students, creators, and buyers across university campuses. Sparkle combines real-time messaging, video moments, campus discovery, and a full-featured campus marketplace into one seamless experience.

## Core Sparkle Features
- **Sparkle Marketplace**: Buy, sell, and discover pre-loved tech, textbooks, subleases, and campus living essentials.
- **Direct & Group Messaging**: Fast, end-to-end user-to-user messaging with rich media, audio notes, Giphy integration, and reply previews.
- **Moments & Stories**: Share 15-second ephemeral video moments and daily campus stories.
- **Sparkle Connect & Campus Discovery**: Discover creators and campus peers by university, shared interests, and mutual followers.
- **Sparkly AI Assistant**: Official AI guide for smart marketplace searches, policy questions, and instant answers.
- **Professional Dashboard & Boost**: Monitor listing engagement, track wallet transactions, and boost item visibility across campus.
