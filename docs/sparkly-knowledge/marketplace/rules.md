# Sparkle Marketplace Policies & Rules

Category: Marketplace / Policies

## Prohibited Items & General Rules
Sparkle Marketplace enforces strict safety and legal standards:
- Prohibited items: Illegal substances, weapons, counterfeit goods, stolen property, pirated software, and academic dishonesty materials.
- All prices must be quoted transparently in KES (Kenyan Shillings).
- Fake descriptions, misleading prices, or price manipulation are grounds for instant seller suspension.
- Communication with buyers should happen through Sparkle Marketplace Chat for record safety.

## Safe Meetups
- Always meet in designated safe campus locations (e.g. Student Center, Campus Library entrance, Security Gate).
- Inspect products thoroughly before transferring payment.
- Confirm meetup status via the order confirmation button inside Marketplace Chat.
