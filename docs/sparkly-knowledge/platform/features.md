# Sparkle Platform Features & Help

Category: Platform / Features

## What is Sparkle?
Sparkle is an all-in-one social and peer-to-peer campus marketplace platform built for Kenyan university students and young adults. It integrates social networking, messaging, campus communities, and student commerce into a unified mobile and web experience.

## Core Features
1. **Marketplace**: Peer-to-peer commerce for buying and selling electronics, clothing, books, dorm essentials, and services with verified campus members. All listings use Kenyan Shillings (KES).
2. **Messages & Chat**: Direct 1-on-1 messaging, group chats, voice notes, stickers, disappearing media, and AI shopping assistant integration (@sparkle).
3. **Moments & Stories**: Short-form video reels, campus moments, and 24-hour disappearing stories.
4. **Campus Hub & Clubs**: Connect with students across Kenyan universities including University of Nairobi (UoN), Kenyatta University (KU), Strathmore, JKUAT, USIU, Moi University, and more.
5. **Confessions**: Anonymous campus confessions with community reactions.
6. **Lost & Found**: Report lost or recovered student IDs, gadgets, and personal belongings.
7. **Sparkle Pay / Wallet**: In-app digital wallet supporting secure M-Pesa deposits, peer transfers, and marketplace transactions.
8. **Sparkly AI Assistant**: In-app AI shopping and platform assistant capable of searching marketplace listings, comparing prices, answering platform questions, and researching external information.
