# Sparkle Referral Program & Invites

Category: Referrals / Rewards

## How to Invite Friends to Sparkle
1. Tap the **Invite / Refer Friends** banner in your dashboard or menu.
2. Share your personalized Sparkle referral link or referral code with classmates and friends.
3. When a friend signs up with your code, verifies their student profile, or makes their first marketplace transaction, both of you earn rewards.

## Referral Rewards
- Referral bonuses and boost credits are automatically credited to your Sparkle Wallet.
- Check your referral stats in the Invite tab to see your total invites, pending activations, and earned rewards in KES.
