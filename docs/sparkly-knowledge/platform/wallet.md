# Sparkle Pay & Wallet Guide

Category: Payments / Wallet

## What is Sparkle Pay?
Sparkle Pay is the integrated payment system within Sparkle. It allows users to safely pay for marketplace purchases, tip creators, split bills with campus friends, and withdraw funds directly to M-Pesa.

## Adding Money (Deposit)
1. Open the **Wallet** tab from the main menu or profile.
2. Tap **Add Money / Top Up**.
3. Enter your M-Pesa phone number and amount in KES.
4. An STK push prompt will appear on your phone. Enter your M-Pesa PIN to complete the deposit.

## Marketplace Escrow & Safe Payments
- When you purchase an item via Sparkle Marketplace, funds are held securely until the buyer inspects and confirms receipt of the product.
- Always meet in designated safe campus locations before releasing payment.
- Never accept off-platform cash requests if you want escrow buyer protection.

## Withdrawals to M-Pesa
1. Go to Wallet > **Withdraw**.
2. Enter the amount to transfer to your registered Safaricom M-Pesa line.
3. Withdrawals are processed instantly via automated payment gateways.
