# How to Sell on Sparkle Marketplace

Category: Marketplace / Selling

## Listing Creation & Guidelines
To sell an item on Sparkle Marketplace:
1. Navigate to Marketplace and tap the **+ Create Listing** button.
2. Upload clear photos of your product (up to 20 images supported).
3. Set an accurate title, detailed description, condition (New, Like New, Good, Fair), and price in KES.
4. Select your primary campus (e.g. Main Campus, Kenyatta University, University of Nairobi, Strathmore, etc.) and safe meetup location.
5. Publish your listing.

## Verification & Trust
- Verified campus members get higher visibility.
- Provide accurate specs and photos to avoid order disputes.
- Sparkly Bot will help buyers discover your listings when they ask for products in your category.
