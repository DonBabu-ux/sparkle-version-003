# Account, Profile & Security Settings on Sparkle

Category: Account / Settings

## How to Change Your Username
1. Open your Profile tab in the Sparkle app.
2. Tap **Edit Profile**.
3. Under the **Username** field, enter your desired new username (must be unique, alphanumeric and underscores only).
4. Tap **Save Changes**. Note: Username changes are subject to a cooldown period to prevent identity confusion.

## How to Change Your Display Name
1. Go to Profile > **Edit Profile**.
2. Update the **Name / Display Name** field.
3. Tap **Save Changes**.

## Account Verification (Blue Badge)
Verified badges identify official campus student leaders, registered student businesses, or verified creators:
1. Navigate to Settings > **Account Verification**.
2. Submit a photo of your valid student ID card or national ID.
3. Applications are reviewed by Sparkle campus moderators within 24-48 hours.

## Privacy & Safety
- **Two-Factor Authentication (2FA)**: Enable 2FA in Settings > Security to protect your login with SMS / PIN verification.
- **Blocking & Reporting**: Tap the three dots on any user's profile to block or report them for spam, harassment, or fraudulent listings.
- **Account Deletion**: Located in Settings > Danger Zone. Deleting your account permanently schedules removal of your profile, posts, and listings after a 14-day grace period.
